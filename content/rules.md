# Server rules

1. **Be respectful.** No harassment, hate speech, personal attacks or discrimination of any kind.
2. **Write in English** in the public channels, so that everyone can follow.
3. **No spam or advertising.** That includes unsolicited direct messages to other members.
4. **No piracy.** Do not share or ask for cracked copies of the game or of paid content.
5. **Use the right channel** and keep long off-topic discussions out of the mod channels.
6. **Report bugs on GitHub.** Discord is for questions and discussion; the issue trackers are where bugs get fixed.
7. **No NSFW content.**
8. **Follow the Discord Terms of Service and Community Guidelines.**

The maintainers may remove content and members that break these rules. If something is wrong, contact a {{@Maintainer}}.
