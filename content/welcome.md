# Welcome to Gregtorio & ME Network

This is the community server for two Factorio mods.

**Gregtorio Continued** is a GregTech-style total overhaul that follows the GregTech: New Horizons progression, from digging clay with a shovel up to the stargate. It continues Gregtorio by Damien Reave.
Mod portal: https://mods.factorio.com/mod/gregtorio-continued
Source and issues: https://github.com/Rykon00/Gregtorio

**ME Network** is a storage and logistics network inspired by Applied Energistics 2: cables, drives, terminals, buses, autocrafting and fluids. It works on its own and is also a dependency of Gregtorio Continued.
Mod portal: https://mods.factorio.com/mod/me-network
Source and issues: https://github.com/Rykon00/me-network

## Found a bug?
Bugs are tracked on GitHub. Open an issue in the repository of the mod it belongs to and attach your log file and, if you can, a save. If you are not sure whether something is a bug, ask here first.
